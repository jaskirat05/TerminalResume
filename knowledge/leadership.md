# Leadership & Technical Contributions

## ComfyUI — Cloud & MCP Ecosystem

Worked closely with the **ComfyUI ecosystem** around its early cloud offering and MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration, contributing to the intersection of **generative AI infrastructure, workflow orchestration, model serving, and agentic interfaces**.

* Worked with **ComfyUI's initial cloud offering**, gaining hands-on experience with cloud-hosted generative AI workflows and the infrastructure required to expose node-based AI pipelines as scalable services.
* Worked extensively with **ComfyUI workflows and custom nodes**, designing and adapting complex generative AI pipelines for production-oriented applications.
* Explored the architecture and operational requirements of running **ComfyUI workloads in cloud GPU environments**, including model loading, workflow execution, GPU resource utilization, inference performance, and workload management.
* Worked with the **ComfyUI MCP integration**, connecting ComfyUI's generative workflow capabilities with the emerging **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** ecosystem.
* Worked on exposing generative AI capabilities through **MCP-based interfaces**, enabling AI agents and LLM-powered systems to interact with ComfyUI workflows programmatically.
* Explored the use of **MCP as an agent-to-tool interface**, allowing models to discover and invoke capabilities exposed by generative AI infrastructure.
* Worked at the intersection of **MCP, LLM agents, tool calling, structured inputs/outputs, workflow orchestration, and generative AI execution**.
* Designed workflows with the goal of making complex **image and video generation pipelines accessible to agentic systems** rather than requiring direct manual interaction with the underlying UI.
* Worked with concepts around **agent harnesses, service-based agents, tool discovery, tool execution, context management, structured outputs, and autonomous workflow execution**.
* Investigated how node-based generative workflows can be transformed into **reusable, composable AI services** that can be orchestrated by LLM-based agents.
* Worked across the boundary between **generative model infrastructure and agent infrastructure**, focusing on how AI agents can reliably invoke computationally expensive image and video generation workloads.
* Applied knowledge of **LLM inference, generative AI pipelines, GPU workloads, workflow orchestration, APIs, MCP, and cloud infrastructure** to build more composable AI systems.
* Explored the broader implications of MCP for **AI-native tooling**, particularly the ability for agents to interact with specialized capabilities such as image generation, video generation, model inference, and creative workflows.
* Contributed to the development mindset around making AI capabilities **discoverable, callable, composable, and interoperable across agentic systems**.
