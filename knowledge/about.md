## About Me

I’m a **Generative AI Engineer and AI Infrastructure Engineer** focused on building, optimizing, and deploying modern **AI/ML systems across LLMs, multimodal models, video generation, computer vision, agentic systems, and scalable inference infrastructure**.

My work spans the full AI stack—from **model architecture and attention mechanisms to inference, optimization, serving, orchestration, and production deployment**. I work with **Large Language Models (LLMs), Vision-Language Models (VLMs), diffusion models, video generation models, multimodal architectures, and 3D/vision systems**, with a strong focus on making computationally intensive models practical and efficient.

### LLMs, Transformers & Model Optimization

I work extensively with **Transformer architectures, self-attention, cross-attention, multi-head attention, FlashAttention, efficient attention, KV caching, positional embeddings, RoPE, context optimization, speculative decoding, batching, quantization, mixed-precision inference, GPU acceleration, memory optimization, and inference profiling**.

My interests include optimizing the entire inference path for **latency, throughput, memory footprint, GPU utilization, tokens/sec, time-to-first-token (TTFT), cost per inference, and overall serving efficiency**. I’m particularly interested in understanding and optimizing the interaction between **model architecture, kernels, GPU memory, compute utilization, and serving infrastructure**.

### Generative AI & Video Models

Beyond language models, I work with **generative video and multimodal AI**, including **video diffusion models, image-to-video, text-to-video, video-to-video, temporal modeling, spatial-temporal attention, motion conditioning, video transformers, latent diffusion, diffusion transformers (DiT), VAE pipelines, denoising optimization, and long-context video generation**.

I’m also interested in the optimization challenges specific to video models—where **sequence length, temporal attention, VRAM consumption, attention complexity, inference latency, and temporal consistency** become significant engineering constraints.

My work also extends into **3D-aware generation, facial modeling, lip synchronization, digital humans, and avatar systems**, including technologies and research areas such as **3DMM (3D Morphable Models), LipDub, face reenactment, facial animation, audio-driven animation, speech-to-face generation, and multimodal avatar pipelines**.

### Agentic AI & AI Systems

I build **service-based agents and agentic systems** designed around modular, composable AI services rather than single monolithic applications. I’m interested in **agent architectures, agent harnesses, tool calling, function calling, structured outputs, planning, reasoning loops, memory, context management, orchestration, autonomous workflows, browser agents, multi-agent systems, and reliable tool execution**.

I’m particularly interested in the engineering layer behind agents: **agent harness design, sandboxing, tool execution, state management, observability, evaluation, failure recovery, context optimization, and scalable agent infrastructure**.

### World Models & Multimodal Intelligence

I’m also exploring **world models, embodied AI, multimodal reasoning, video understanding, temporal representation learning, environment modeling, predictive modeling, and vision-language-action systems**.

I’m interested in systems that move beyond generating individual outputs toward **learning representations of environments, understanding temporal dynamics, predicting future states, and interacting with complex environments**.

### AI Infrastructure & Inference

On the infrastructure side, I work with **GPU compute, cloud infrastructure, model serving, distributed inference, containerization, orchestration, resource allocation, sandboxed execution, microVMs, and scalable AI workloads**.

I’m particularly interested in the intersection of **ML systems and systems engineering**—optimizing how models actually execute on hardware and how inference workloads are scheduled, served, monitored, and scaled.
